# Source notes and content verification

Reviewed 9 November 2026. This document separates verified public-site content from design choices.

## Facts taken from City Gym's public pages

- Brand headline: "Australia's Most Iconic & Inclusive Gym" (homepage).
- Membership entry price advertised: "From $24.95 p/w" (homepage and Join Now page).
- Homepage says a free pass is available for Australian residents only.
- Classes page says City Gym offers unlimited classes across multiple types.
- Classes named on the public classes page: City Spin, Reformer Pilates, City HIIT, Mat Pilates, City Strength, City Box, Livestrong, Yoga Zen Ki, Yoga Vinyasa, Yoga Hatha and Yoga Flow.
- Facilities on Explore: Booty Section, Cardio Theatre, Functional Training, Mind & Body and Weights.
- Weights page snippet says the weights floor has over 150 equipment pieces from renowned brands.
- Contact details: 107–113 Crown Street, Darlinghurst NSW 2010; 02 9360 6247; reception@citygym.com.au; sales@citygym.com.au; groupfitness@citygym.com.au.

## Deliberately not included

- Opening hours: homepage and contact page use different wording. Confirm before publishing.
- Membership tiers, inclusions, current campaign end dates and detailed prices: not all were available as text in the crawl; the concept only states the advertised starting price.
- Testimonials, member counts, star ratings, transformation stats, trainer qualifications and performance guarantees: no unsupported claims added.
- Official logo and original City Gym photography: not copied into this concept.

# City Gym — single-page redesign concept

This is an independent front-end concept prepared as a cold-outreach preview for City Gym Australia. It is not the official City Gym website and is not affiliated with City Gym or its current website agency.

## Run locally

Open `index.html` in a modern browser. The page uses plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript; no build step or package manager is required.

## What is included

- Responsive single-page landing page
- City Gym-inspired red, black, white and metallic-grey visual system
- Mobile navigation and scroll-reveal motion
- Sections for brand positioning, facilities, classes, eatery, coaches, equipment brands, membership and contact
- Links to City Gym's official pages for live membership, class, story and contact information
- Uses public City Gym logo, selected public coach portraits and equipment-brand assets

## Important production notes

- No generic Unsplash placeholders remain. Facility and eatery photographs are genuine City Gym venue images surfaced in public editorial coverage from Broadsheet and RitKeep; they are hotlinked from those publishers, not City Gym-hosted files.
- The official City Gym logo is hotlinked from City Gym’s public WordPress media library.
- The `from $24.95 p/w` price is reproduced from City Gym's public website as observed on 9 November 2026. Confirm the current offer, inclusions and terms with the club before publication.
- City Gym's public homepage and contact page display inconsistent opening-hours wording. Opening hours are intentionally omitted; ask the club to confirm them.
- Links to membership, class and contact pages point to City Gym's official website. No payment, booking, membership or contact backend is included.
- The homepage says the free pass is available for Australian residents only. Eligibility and terms should be confirmed directly with City Gym.
- All claims should be reviewed and approved by City Gym before launch.
